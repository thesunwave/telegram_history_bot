# Dutch Rudder achievement

## Why

Reward two participants who take turns drawing other people into each other's conversations.

## What Changes

- Award a secret personal badge to both participants after they swap roles in a group.
- Reveal the award in both personal albums and permit each owner to share the card.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `paired-conversation-achievements` — recognize two participants from the existing consented reply graph.

## Impact

Uses the current 28-day reply evidence and awards table. Adds one bundled illustration and requires no database migration.
